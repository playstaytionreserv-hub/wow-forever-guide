Entry No. 001

Why Forever?

World of Warcraft has traditionally moved forward by leaving old chapters behind. WoW Forever presents a different idea: expanding Azeroth while allowing the journey that came before to continue meaning something.

01

Azeroth as a Place, Not a Season

One of the most appealing ideas behind a persistent version of World of Warcraft is that the world itself can remain important. Characters do not need to feel temporary, professions can develop over time, and returning to an older area does not automatically mean returning to irrelevant content.

“Progression becomes more interesting when the destination does not erase the journey.”

Chapter II

Raids & Dungeons

Endgame encounters provide long-term goals for players who enjoy learning mechanics, improving characters and overcoming difficult content together.

Chapter III

Professions

Crafting and gathering feel more meaningful when recipes, materials and profession investments continue to have a place within the world.

Chapter IV

PvP

A stable environment gives competitive players more time to learn matchups, experiment with classes and improve without immediately preparing for another complete reset.

What Happens When the World Keeps Growing?

The real challenge for WoW Forever is not simply adding more content. It is finding ways to expand Azeroth without making yesterday's accomplishments meaningless.

A new raid can exist alongside an older one. A profession can gain new recipes without losing its identity. Alternate characters can become long-term projects instead of temporary experiments.

If that balance works, progression could feel less like a sequence of resets and more like a history that belongs to the character.
